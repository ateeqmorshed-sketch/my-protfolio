Our family

One recipe book,
three generations.

It started with Dada's hand-crank churner at the farmers' market in 1987. Then Mom and Dad opened the shop and added the flavours the neighbourhood asked for. Now the grandkids run the counter every summer.

Everything is still made the slow way: small batches, Alberta cream, real fruit — and the recipe book stays in the kitchen drawer where it's always been.
