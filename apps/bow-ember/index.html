Our story

Named for the river.
Run on the fire.

The Bow gives Calgary its shape; the ember is where prairie cooking has always happened. We cook Alberta's own larder — beef raised an hour from the city, Red Fife wheat, Hutterite chickens, Saskatoon berries — over birch and dried applewood.

No freezer theatrics, no imported hype. A short menu, done over fire, changed with the seasons.

"Everything on the plate grew, grazed or swam within 300 km of this room."
300 km promise

Every core ingredient sourced within 300 km of Calgary — named farms, printed on the menu.

Fire, not fryers

A six-foot open hearth is the whole kitchen. Birch coals, cast iron, patience.

Everyone at the table
