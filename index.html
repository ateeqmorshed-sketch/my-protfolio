    Questions, answered

    Before you ask.

    How much does a website or app cost?

    Every project gets a fixed price before any work starts, based on a free scoping call — no hourly surprises, no meters running. Because I work solo with a lean toolchain, my quotes routinely come in well under agency prices for the same result.

    How fast can it be live?

    Simple business websites go live in 2–4 days. Booking sites take under a week, online stores about a week. Full business systems like the factory platform take a few weeks — but you'll click a working preview within 48 hours, whatever the size.

    Who owns the website when it's done?

    You do — completely. The code, the domain, the hosting account, the admin logins. I set everything up in your name and hand it over documented, so you're never locked in or held hostage for changes.

    Can I update things myself, or do I have to call you?

    Where it makes sense, you get an admin panel — the fragrance shop updates its own products and prices, the factory manages its own orders and staff. For everything else, small changes are quick, and I'm one message away.

    Do you only work with Calgary businesses?

    Calgary is home, but the work ships worldwide — I've built for businesses in Canada and Bangladesh, and everything happens online anyway. Local clients get in-person meetings; everyone gets the same product.

    What if I already have a website but it's not bringing customers?

    Send me the link — I'll reply with a free, no-strings check-up: three concrete things holding it back and what fixing them would involve. If you want me to do the fixing, great; if not, the advice is yours to keep.
